About FliccPicker

Can't decide what to watch on Netflix? Overwhelmed by too many choices? FliccPicker is here to help. Pick a genre and we'll surface a curated selection of movies — one click takes you straight to Netflix to start watching.

FliccPicker is a lightweight, front-end-only web app built with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and jQuery. Movie data is filtered locally and your favorites are saved right in your browser.
